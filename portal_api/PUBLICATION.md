# Publication and verification

New documents use `draft_first` delivery. Bounded author-intent corrections and verification still run, but unresolved evidence and coverage findings are stored in `generator_version_reviews` and exposed only through owner-authenticated version-history notes. HTML, paginated previews and PDF contain no added verification markers, warning banners or evidence-audit appendices. The internal WARNING outcome does not certify accuracy. Invalid citation attributions are removed before export; known fabricated verbatim quotations and inconsistent calculations are not released. Legacy approved files retain their original rendering and approval binding.

Recovery of a failed draft can promote its saved repaired text without repeating paid chapter calls. Production-brief scaffolding is excluded from reader exports when a saved subject chapter is available. Original candidates, diagnostics and the original brief remain retained. Explicit page plans in new production briefs drive the outline; editorial/source rules remain constraints rather than mandatory prose chapters. Page-count shortfalls are recorded in private history after PDF export.

The strict legacy policy below remains available for historical jobs and regression checks.

Generation retains the immutable author brief and intent contract. Missing independent factual evidence does not authorize a change to the author's argument.

`generation_verification.py` classifies the independent review separately from document integrity:

- **PASS**: structural, intent, source and calculation checks pass.
- **WARNING**: a complete draft contains factual assertions requiring verification. Accuracy is not certified (`passed: false`). The renderer adds claim markers, a cover label, running-header labels and the exact outstanding issues. HTML and PDF are delivered as `needs_review`, without waiting for final-HTML approval.
- **BLOCKED**: fabricated citations/quotations, contradictory calculations, missing required content, substantive drift or unusable output. The interface disables unchanged recovery. Corrected content, evidence or criteria must change the fingerprint before a fresh review can run.

Pure authorial proposals are not empirical claims. Author-sourced empirical statements and regulatory assertions require independent evidence; citing the original brief is not independent verification. Source quotations remain checked exactly. Untraceable model-generated numbers, invalid empirical graphics and fabricated citations are not downgraded to warnings. The calculator is re-run before publication, including when resuming from saved assembly state.

New documents use the selected Databricks model for claim extraction, assessment, correction and independent publication review. Tavily Search/Extract supply retrieval only, with generated answers and automatic search-depth changes disabled. Each search round can follow up to five existing official links from its downloaded pages, selected by the same model, to retrieve controlling rules and guidance. This is one hop, with no invented URLs or recursive crawl. Search choices are saved per claim scope and round, so changed assessment wording cannot introduce a third search during recovery. Regulatory assertions require controlling rules plus relevant official guidance at distinct official URLs. Other assertions require appropriate primary sources. Exact source quotations, scope and date checks are mandatory; snippets alone are not evidence. Legacy jobs keep their saved graph and recovery behavior.

Publication review identities bind manuscript, rendered assets, source packet, required criteria and validation policy version. Raw reviews are checkpointed before decision handling. A legacy rejected assembly checkpoint can adopt its exact completed reviewer responses once, decoding them through the original response decoder. Subsequent changes invalidate that binding. No writing chapters or completed provider responses are discarded.

A document-level research ledger shares an initial estimated US$5 allowance across versions and retries. Requests reserve funds transactionally; Databricks token costs use matching-model published prices plus a 50% buffer, while Tavily credit usage is valued at pay-as-you-go rates. These are estimates, not a guarantee of the workspace invoice. Unknown outcomes retain reservations and cannot be resubmitted automatically. Missing prices pause new paid work. Budget increases change the explicit total allowance, never reset it.

Incomplete or unbound independent-review responses cannot certify publication, but do not by themselves establish a manuscript integrity failure. Intact manuscripts remain available as WARNING drafts. Explicit critical findings and deterministic citation, calculation and completeness failures stay BLOCKED. Historical artifact checks use their saved validation-policy versions; new work and recovery use the current criteria.

Verification-only revisions copy the saved manuscript, create the next version, and reuse unchanged operations and evidence without repeating chapter generation. Each claim group has two search rounds, at most one factual corrective edit, and one independent recheck. Supported/corrected claims clear only source-bound, unchanged blocks; unresolved claims keep visible qualifications. Corrections and their evidence appear in the interface and both document formats. Existing chapter repairs remain bounded. Serious unresolved defects remain blocked rather than cycling through paid retries. Historical approved artifacts retain their original fingerprint and HTML hash; rendering a new artifact requires the new gate.

`tests/test_verification_delivery.py` covers the Vancouver/California regulatory rejection, both downloadable formats, repeated recovery without new provider requests, evidence-sensitive cache invalidation, optional primary-source retrieval, proposals versus facts, and critical failures. `tests/check_verification_delivery.py` checks the warning interface and paginated preview at 1440/375/320/430 pixels.

`tests/test_research.py` covers the selected-model boundary, factual corrections, source scope/date failures, shared budgets, concurrent reservations and uncertain submissions. `tests/check_research.py` verifies research controls, evidence and correction reports, explicit allowance increases and verification revisions at 1440/375/320/430 pixels. Tavily secrets remain server-side; the credential-bearing MCP URL is not used in browser responses or the production retrieval transport.
