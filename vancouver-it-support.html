How we help

Add capacity where it makes a difference.

We work alongside your business or IT team on a defined technical task. Start with the systems involved, the problem you need to solve, and the outcome that would make the work useful. We agree on scope and responsibilities before implementation, then keep the work understandable through documentation and handover.

What a project can include

  • Data lake setup, database transfers, and integration between existing applications.
  • Reporting support, Power BI data models, and refresh troubleshooting.
  • Workflow automation, API development, and focused internal tools.
  • Technical investigation, implementation, and a documented handover to your team.

A practical example

Extra delivery capacity for a local IT business

For example, an IT provider may have a client who needs a recurring operations report built from several databases. Nexusflow can scope the data connections, build the transformation workflow, and hand over the reporting setup while the provider continues managing its client relationship.

Planning your project

Bring one task, a rough deadline, and the systems involved. We will identify the access, dependencies, and delivery steps needed. Our support focuses on scoped technical projects; service hours and any ongoing support arrangements are agreed for the work.

Useful questions

Can you help with a small project?

Yes. A single report, database transfer, or workflow fix is a useful starting point. The project can stay focused or grow into a broader integration when there is a clear reason.

Do you work with local IT businesses and international clients?

Yes. We are particularly effective at supporting local IT businesses that need focused technical delivery. From Vancouver, we also support international clients across Asia and beyond, agreeing on communication and handover arrangements for each project.
