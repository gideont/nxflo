How we help

Build a dependable path from source to target.

The pipeline can use database connections, APIs, or files, depending on what your systems support. Useful questions

What is the difference between ETL and ELT?

ETL transforms data before loading it into the target. ELT loads first and performs transformations in the destination platform. We choose the approach to fit the systems, data volume, and operating requirements.

Can the pipeline run on a schedule or support periodic backups?

Yes, a transfer can be scheduled and used for periodic data copies. When the goal is backup, we also scope retention, consistency, and restoration requirements so a successful transfer is not mistaken for a complete recovery plan.
